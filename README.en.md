## Prerequisites

1. Recommended: open the notebook in Google Colab.

   [![Open In Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/assets/colab-badge.svg)](https://colab.research.google.com/github/kishiyamat/jcss-2022-repl/blob/main/analysis_en.ipynb)

1. Obtain the CSV files from the [data folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1t1rN5V0BJJCMeq3Gu_WSjddd71kUOLXM?usp=sharing); access requires a request. In Colab, use the upload cell in the notebook to place the files in `data/`.
1. Alternative: use the [Binder environment](https://binder.cs.rcos.nii.ac.jp/v2/gh/kishiyamat/jcss-2022-repl/main). See the [documentation](https://meatwiki.nii.ac.jp/confluence/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=67614937) for details.
